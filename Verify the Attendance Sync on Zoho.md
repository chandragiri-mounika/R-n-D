
# Zoho People – Attendance Verification SOP

## Purpose
To ensure employee attendance data is properly synced and verified in Zoho People.

## Step 1: Log in to Zoho People

1. Open the [Zoho People](https://people.zoho.com) portal.
2. Sign in using your corporate credentials (Office 365 or Zoho credentials).

## Step 2: Navigate to Attendance

1. From the left-side panel, click on **Attendance**.
2. You will see the following two tabs:
   - **Attendance Summary**
   - **On Duty**
3. Ensure that the **Attendance Summary** tab is selected.

---

**Note:** Use the Attendance Summary section to review employee attendance records and verify that the data is properly synced in Zoho People.
